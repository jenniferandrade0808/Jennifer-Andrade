# AI Conventions — Jennifer Andrade

This file tells any AI assistant working in this repository how I work and what I expect.
`CLAUDE.md` points here so any tool finds it automatically.

## Who I am
Retired U.S. Air Force Captain (23 years of service), now a licensed Nurse Practitioner.
Currently enrolled in the dual-track Executive MBA / Doctorate of Nursing Practice program
at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa (Fall 2026 start). Board member and lead medical
trainer for Kauaʻi Search and Rescue.

## How I like to work with AI
- Draft first, then I edit until it sounds like me — first drafts are a starting point, not a deliverable.
- Flag assumptions instead of silently picking one.
- Keep language direct and concrete; avoid filler and hedging.

## Standards for this repo
- Every deliverable folder under `capabilities/` needs a `README.md`, `spec.md`, and the
  supporting file(s) — no folder ships empty.
- Decision memos go in `docs/decisions/` with: what was decided, why, who decided it, and
  what would reverse it.
- Briefs (scope + hypothesis) go in `docs/briefs/` *before* the work starts.
- Commit messages describe what changed, never "update" or "fix."

## What must never be committed
- Credentials, API keys, `.env` files
- Any patient-identifiable health information
- Anything covered in `.gitignore`

## AI disclosure
When AI materially helped draft a file, I add a one-line disclosure at the bottom:
"Drafted with help from Claude (Anthropic, 2026); reviewed and edited by me."
